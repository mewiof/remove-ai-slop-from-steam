# Privacy

Remove AI Slop from Steam collects no data.

- It only runs on store.steampowered.com.
- To rate a game, it sends the game's app ID to Steam's store API at api.steampowered.com. The request goes from your browser to Steam. Nothing is sent to the extension's developer or to anyone else.
- Settings/the games you chose to always show/cached disclosures stay in the browser's extension storage. Removing the extension deletes them.
- No analytics/ads/remote code.
